The same commercial air curtains can provide environmental separation during warmer weather. In an air-conditioned shop, office, hotel, restaurant or other commercial building, cooled indoor air can escape whenever an external doorway remains open. At the same time, warmer external air can enter the premises. A commercial air curtain helps separate these temperature zones while allowing unrestricted access.

Flexiheat UK classifies its commercial air curtains as suitable for horizontal installation at heights of up to approximately 4 metres, depending on the specific model and heating configuration. This makes the commercial air curtain range suitable for many retail and commercial entrances where a standard smaller doorway heater may not have sufficient air throw.

During winter, the heating system maintains the required internal temperature while the commercial air curtain helps prevent some of that heated air from escaping. During summer, the air-conditioning system cools the premises while the commercial air curtain helps retain the conditioned air.

The W commercial air curtain uses a two-row high-performance water heat exchanger. Hot water supplied from the building's heating system passes through the heat exchanger, transferring heat into the airflow produced by the commercial air curtain. The result is a stream of warm air that combines entrance heating with environmental separation.

Internal building pressure can influence performance as well. Mechanical ventilation systems can create positive or negative pressure, changing the way air naturally moves through an open doorway. These factors should be considered where they have a meaningful effect on the commercial air curtain installation.

A commercial air curtain should therefore be viewed as one component of a wider energy management strategy. It works alongside the building's heating, ventilation and air-conditioning systems rather than replacing them.

doorway air barrier

Correct commercial air curtain selection begins with understanding the doorway. The entrance width, mounting height, installation position and external conditions should be established before selecting the required model.

The principle behind commercial air curtains is based on controlled airflow. Air is drawn into the commercial air curtain and discharged at sufficient velocity to create a stream extending across the entrance. When a commercial air curtain is installed horizontally above a doorway, the airflow travels down towards floor level. When commercial air curtains are installed vertically, the airflow travels horizontally across the opening. In either configuration, the objective is to create a continuous air barrier between the internal and external environments.

Water flow through the commercial air curtain can be controlled through an on/off valve system. This allows the heating function to respond to the requirements of the commercial premises rather than operating continuously when heating is unnecessary.

Commercial air curtain sound levels are another consideration, particularly for hospitality, retail and office applications. Fans need to move enough air to establish an effective barrier, but excessive noise can be undesirable in customer-facing premises.

Commercial offices and reception areas have similar requirements. Staff, visitors, contractors and deliveries may pass through the main entrance throughout the working day. Commercial air curtains can reduce the amount of conditioned indoor air lost during this repeated movement.

Flexiheat UK commercial air curtains are available in three primary configurations. Businesses can choose water-heated commercial air curtains, electrically heated commercial air curtains or ambient commercial air curtains without heating elements. Each configuration creates an air barrier, but the treatment of the airflow differs according to the heating requirements of the premises.
