Horizontal and vertical installation options allow commercial air curtains to be adapted to different building layouts. A conventional retail doorway may be suitable for a commercial air curtain installed directly above the entrance, while a wide commercial doorway may be better served by vertical commercial air curtains installed on either side.

Vertical commercial air curtains extend the possible applications to wider entrances, with arrangements capable of covering openings up to approximately 8 metres when units are installed on both sides.

Modern commercial air curtains are designed to balance airflow performance with controlled sound power levels. Selecting the correct commercial air curtain rather than operating an undersized unit continuously at maximum output can also contribute to more appropriate operating conditions.

Commercial air curtains are sometimes known as commercial air doors because the airflow creates environmental separation across a doorway without requiring a solid physical barrier. Customers, employees, visitors, deliveries and equipment can continue moving freely through an entrance while the commercial air curtain operates. This makes commercial air curtains particularly suitable for premises where external doors are frequently opened or where businesses prefer to maintain an open-door policy.

Appearance may influence selection when commercial air curtains are installed in visible locations. Hotels, restaurants, shops and reception areas generally want entrance equipment that complements the building rather than dominating it. Compact commercial air curtains with a modern design can provide the required functionality while remaining relatively discreet.

Sports centres and leisure facilities can experience particularly high entrance traffic before and after classes, matches or events. Commercial air curtains provide environmental separation without introducing physical barriers that could slow visitor movement.

Commercial air curtains create a controlled barrier across the shop entrance. Customers can continue walking through the doorway while the commercial air curtain helps reduce the amount of warm air escaping and cold air entering.

Commercial air curtains can additionally reduce the movement of flying insects through open restaurant entrances. The continuous stream of air provides an additional obstacle for insects attempting to enter the building. Commercial air curtains can therefore complement the wider hygiene and environmental controls used within food service premises.

Commercial air curtains provide businesses with an effective method of separating indoor and outdoor environments while keeping entrances accessible throughout normal operating hours. Flexiheat UK supplies commercial air curtains designed for retail shops, restaurants, hotels, commercial offices, reception areas, supermarkets, sports centres, warehouses, food service premises and many other commercial applications. By producing a controlled stream of air across a doorway, commercial air curtains establish an invisible air barrier that can help reduce heat loss, retain conditioned air, limit cold draughts and reduce the movement of dust, debris and flying insects through an open entrance.

unheated air curtain

For commercial premises with open or frequently used external entrances, commercial air curtains provide a practical method of reducing heat loss and temperature exchange while maintaining easy access. Correctly specified Flexiheat UK commercial air curtains can support energy-efficient building operation, comfortable indoor conditions and effective separation between indoor and outdoor environments.

Heated commercial air curtains can provide a warmer welcome at restaurant entrances during colder conditions. The airflow can help reduce cold draughts while the heating function introduces warm air around the doorway.

The air stream also requires sufficient velocity to reach the opposite edge of the entrance. For horizontal commercial air curtains, this means the airflow should reach the floor with enough remaining velocity to maintain the air barrier. The mounting height of commercial air curtains therefore has a direct relationship with their required airflow performance.

Restaurants can also benefit from commercial air curtains. Restaurant entrances can experience continuous movement during busy service periods. Commercial air curtains help reduce temperature changes associated with repeated door opening while allowing customers and staff to move through the doorway without obstruction.

Cold storage areas can also use commercial air curtains. In these environments, the objective is usually to retain cold air rather than heated air. The commercial air curtain creates separation between the colder internal space and the warmer surrounding environment.

Ambient commercial air curtains are useful where environmental separation is required without additional entrance heating. They can be suitable for air-conditioned commercial premises, cold storage environments and other buildings where maintaining the existing internal temperature is more important than introducing additional heat.

over door air curtain

Reducing warm air infiltration can help the refrigeration system maintain the required internal temperature. Commercial air curtains also allow employees and equipment to move through the entrance without the delays associated with repeatedly opening and closing additional physical barriers.

Supermarkets and larger stores frequently have broad customer entrances. The commercial air curtain system should be selected to cover the complete opening. Depending on the doorway dimensions, this may involve multiple horizontally installed commercial air curtains or vertical commercial air curtains positioned at the sides of the entrance.

Butchers, food processing areas and other food-related businesses may therefore consider commercial air curtains as part of their wider entrance management strategy. The air barrier does not replace normal hygiene measures, but it can provide additional environmental separation.

invisible air curtain

The C commercial air curtain is an ambient model. It does not contain electric heating elements or a hot water heat exchanger. Instead, the commercial air curtain draws air from the internal environment and discharges it across the doorway to establish the required air barrier.

The maximum horizontal installation height for the C ambient commercial air curtain is approximately 4 metres. This additional air throw capability can make ambient commercial air curtains suitable for larger entrances where heating is not required.

Flexiheat UK supplies commercial air curtains designed to achieve this combination of environmental separation, heating options and unrestricted access.
