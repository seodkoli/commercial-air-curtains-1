The same commercial air curtains can provide environmental separation during warmer weather. In an air-conditioned shop, office, hotel, restaurant or other commercial building, cooled indoor air can escape whenever an external doorway remains open. At the same time, warmer external air can enter the premises. A commercial air curtain helps separate these temperature zones while allowing unrestricted access.

Flexiheat UK classifies its commercial air curtains as suitable for horizontal installation at heights of up to approximately 4 metres, depending on the specific model and heating configuration. This makes the commercial air curtain range suitable for many retail and commercial entrances where a standard smaller doorway heater may not have sufficient air throw.

During winter, the heating system maintains the required internal temperature while the commercial air curtain helps prevent some of that heated air from escaping. During summer, the air-conditioning system cools the premises while the commercial air curtain helps retain the conditioned air.

Commercial air curtain sound levels are another consideration, particularly for hospitality, retail and office applications. Fans need to move enough air to establish an effective barrier, but excessive noise can be undesirable in customer-facing premises.

Commercial offices and reception areas have similar requirements. Staff, visitors, contractors and deliveries may pass through the main entrance throughout the working day. Commercial air curtains can reduce the amount of conditioned indoor air lost during this repeated movement.

When talking about commercial air curtains, the dimensions of the entrance are among the first factors that should be considered. The commercial air curtain needs to provide airflow across the complete width and height of the doorway. Selecting a commercial air curtain that does not cover the complete opening can result in areas where external air continues to move freely between the two environments.

Commercial air curtains are also suitable for warehouse and commercial loading applications. Larger doors can allow significant volumes of air to move between the building and the external environment when opened. The larger the opening, the more important appropriate airflow performance becomes.

Electric commercial air curtains can be particularly suitable for premises where an appropriate electrical supply is available or where extending the building's hot water system to the entrance is not practical. Commercial air curtains help reduce this unwanted air exchange. The E commercial air curtain is an electrically heated model. Electric heating elements warm the airflow passing through the commercial air curtain before it is discharged across the entrance. This provides warm air at the doorway without requiring a connection to a hot water heating circuit.

Hotels are another common application. A hotel lobby can experience frequent opening of external doors as guests arrive, leave or move luggage through the entrance. Without environmental separation, these repeated door movements can result in noticeable temperature changes close to reception areas.

Water-heated commercial air curtains require inspection of the heat exchanger, valves and associated water connections. Electric commercial air curtains require appropriate electrical maintenance and inspection of heating components. Ambient commercial air curtains still require regular attention to fans, controls and airflow paths.

surface mounted air curtain

Correct commercial air curtain selection begins with understanding the doorway. The entrance width, mounting height, installation position and external conditions should be established before selecting the required model.

The principle behind commercial air curtains is based on controlled airflow. Air is drawn into the commercial air curtain and discharged at sufficient velocity to create a stream extending across the entrance. When a commercial air curtain is installed horizontally above a doorway, the airflow travels down towards floor level. When commercial air curtains are installed vertically, the airflow travels horizontally across the opening. In either configuration, the objective is to create a continuous air barrier between the internal and external environments.

Water flow through the commercial air curtain can be controlled through an on/off valve system. This allows the heating function to respond to the requirements of the commercial premises rather than operating continuously when heating is unnecessary.

Flexiheat UK commercial air curtains provide options for these different requirements. The commercial air curtain range can accommodate horizontal mounting heights of up to approximately 4 metres with ambient models and approximately 3.7 metres with heated configurations.

Commercial air curtain controls can further support efficient operation. Intelligent controls can adjust fan operation and heating according to demand. This means the commercial air curtain does not necessarily need to operate at maximum output under every condition.

A commercial air curtain designed for a standard shop doorway should not automatically be assumed suitable for a larger warehouse opening. Installation height, width, external wind pressure and internal building pressure should all be considered when specifying commercial air curtains for these applications.
